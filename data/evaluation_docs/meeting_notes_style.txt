Project Governance Meeting - April 2026

The team discussed improvements to payroll diagnostics reporting and retrieval evaluation. A proposal was made to improve document chunking strategies for policy documents and operational manuals.

Discussion also focused on AI retrieval observability, including telemetry logging and unsupported-answer handling.

One concern raised was inconsistent document formatting across client policy packs. The group agreed that ingestion diagnostics should identify low-quality or poorly structured documents before indexing.

The meeting concluded with agreement to evaluate heading chunking versus character chunking across several synthetic test datasets.
