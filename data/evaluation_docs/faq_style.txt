QUESTION:
How do employees request annual leave?

ANSWER:
Employees submit leave requests through the HR portal. Managers review leave balances before approval.

QUESTION:
When is payroll processed?

ANSWER:
Payroll processing occurs every Wednesday afternoon after approvals are completed.

QUESTION:
Who can access payroll records?

ANSWER:
Only authorised payroll, HR, and finance staff may access payroll records.

QUESTION:
What happens during termination processing?

ANSWER:
Termination payments include final wages, leave balances, and superannuation obligations where applicable.

QUESTION:
How long are records retained?

ANSWER:
Payroll records are retained for a minimum of seven years.
