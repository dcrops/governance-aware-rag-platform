SECTION: LEAVE MANAGEMENT

Employees must submit annual leave requests at least two weeks in advance. Managers are responsible for reviewing leave balances before approving requests.

SECTION: PAYROLL APPROVALS

Payroll approval must occur before 3:00 PM each Wednesday. Delayed approvals may result in payment delays.

SECTION: TERMINATION PROCESS

Termination payments must include outstanding leave balances and final ordinary hours worked. Supporting documentation must be retained for audit purposes.

SECTION: RECORD KEEPING

Payroll records must be retained for seven years. Access to payroll records is restricted to authorised personnel only.

SECTION: COMPLIANCE REVIEW

Quarterly payroll governance reviews should assess exception handling, configuration changes, and evidence retention processes.
